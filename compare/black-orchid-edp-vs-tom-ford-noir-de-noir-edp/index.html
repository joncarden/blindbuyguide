Tom Ford Black Orchid Eau de Parfum vs Tom Ford Noir de Noir Eau de Parfum: Tom Ford after dark

Short answer: Black Orchid is the widely available truffle, chocolate and patchouli scent ; Noir de Noir is the Private Blend rose, truffle and oud scent.

Two of Tom Ford's darkest scents. Black Orchid is the famous one, available nearly everywhere; Noir de Noir is the Private Blend cult favorite.

How they differ

  • Black Orchid lists jasmine, black truffle, ylang-ylang and blackcurrant, on patchouli, sandalwood, dark chocolate and incense.
  • Noir de Noir lists saffron, then black rose and truffle, on patchouli, vanilla, oud and treemoss.

Try both first

A 1 ml of each costs $11.98 together. Wear one on each wrist and check at 10 minutes, 2 hours and 6 hours (how to test properly).

Verdict: Black Orchid for the entry-level goth era. Noir de Noir if you are fully in your villain arc and dressing for it. Sample both first.
