How they differ

  • Baccarat Rouge 540 lists saffron and jasmine, then amberwood and ambergris, on fir resin, cedar and sugar.
  • Oud Satin Mood lists rose, violet and strawberry, then Turkish rose, on oud, vanilla, amber and benzoin. Plush and much heavier.

Try both first

A 1 ml of each costs $18.98 together. Wear one on each wrist and check at 10 minutes, 2 hours and 6 hours (how to test properly).

Verdict: BR540 if you want the scent everyone will recognize (for better and worse). Oud Satin Mood if you want to smell like a velvet robe in a Bridgerton ballroom. Sample both.
