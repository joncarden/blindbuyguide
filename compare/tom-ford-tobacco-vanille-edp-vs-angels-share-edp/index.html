Tom Ford Tobacco Vanille Eau de Parfum vs By Kilian Angels' Share Eau de Parfum: the cozy-scent showdown

Short answer: Both are sweet winter scents; Tobacco Vanille leans tobacco and spice, while Angels’ Share leans cognac, cinnamon and praline.

Two of the most-loved boozy, sweet, wintry niche scents. Tobacco Vanille is Tom Ford's; Angels' Share is By Kilian's cognac fantasy.

How they differ

  • Tobacco Vanille lists tobacco leaf and spice, then vanilla, cacao, tonka and tobacco blossom, on dried fruit and woods.
  • Angels' Share lists cognac, then cinnamon, oak and tonka, on praline, vanilla and sandalwood. More dessert, less smoke.

Try both first

A 1 ml of each costs $13.98 together. Wear one on each wrist and check at 10 minutes, 2 hours and 6 hours (how to test properly).

Verdict: Tobacco Vanille if you want a fireplace. Angels’ Share if you want the dessert after the fireplace. Do not buy both in one winter unless you are the main character of a Hallmark movie.
