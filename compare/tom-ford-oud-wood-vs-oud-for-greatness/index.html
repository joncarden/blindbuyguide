Tom Ford Oud Wood vs Initio Parfums Oud for Greatness: the beginner oud vs the beast

Short answer: Oud Wood is the smoother, more beginner-friendly oud with vanilla and amber; Oud for Greatness is bolder with saffron and patchouli.

If you're oud-curious, these two come up first. Oud Wood is the polished intro; Oud for Greatness is the one people warn you about.

How they differ

  • Oud Wood lists rosewood, cardamom and Chinese pepper, then oud, sandalwood and vetiver, on tonka, vanilla and amber. Smooth, office-safe for an oud.
  • Oud for Greatness lists saffron, nutmeg and lavender, then oud, on patchouli and musk. Louder and more of a statement.

Try both first

A 1 ml of each costs $15.98 together. Wear one on each wrist and check at 10 minutes, 2 hours and 6 hours (how to test properly).

Verdict: Oud Wood if you are dipping a toe. Oud for Greatness if you want the whole elevator to know. Sample first; oud is a love-it-or-hate-it note.
