Le Labo Santal 33 Eau de Parfum vs Le Labo Another 13 Eau de Parfum: Le Labo's two cult classics

Santal 33 is the scent of every downtown hotel lobby since 2014. Another 13 is the musky, skin-scent sibling for people who find Santal too loud.

How they differ

  • Santal 33 lists sandalwood, cedar, cardamom, violet, papyrus, leather, amber and iris. Woody and leathery, very recognizable.
  • Another 13 lists pear, apple and citrus, then ambrette, moss and jasmine, on Iso E Super and musks. Subtle and skin-like.

Try both first

A 1 ml of each costs $15.98 together. Wear one on each wrist and check at 10 minutes, 2 hours and 6 hours (how to test properly).

Verdict: Santal 33 if you want people to know. Another 13 if you want them to wonder. Either way, sample first.
