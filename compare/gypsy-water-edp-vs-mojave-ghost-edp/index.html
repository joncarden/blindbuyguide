How they differ

  • Gypsy Water lists bergamot, lemon, pepper and juniper, then incense, pine needles and orris, on amber, vanilla and sandalwood. Woodsy, a campfire in a pine forest.
  • Mojave Ghost lists sapodilla and ambrette, then magnolia, violet and sandalwood, on ambergris, cedar and musk. Soft, airy, skin-like.

Try both first

A 1 ml of each costs $13.98 together. Wear one on each wrist and check at 10 minutes, 2 hours and 6 hours (how to test properly).

Verdict: Gypsy Water for the cabin weekend, Mojave Ghost for every day you want to smell like expensive skin. Sample both first.
