## 4. Funnel math

Every rate below is an **assumption** based on typical content-affiliate sites, not something we've measured.

| Assumption | Value |
|---|---|
| Visitors who click any shop link | 10% |
| Clickers who buy | 4% |
| Average commission per order | $3 (sample orders of about $15 to $25 at about 10%; occasional bottle at 4% to 5%) |
| Revenue per 1,000 visitors | 1,000 × 10% × 4% × $3 = **$12** |

**Where we are.** At about 8 real visitors a day (~250 a month), affiliate revenue would be about $3 a month even with every link tracked. Today it is close to $0, since 69% of the main buttons are untracked.

**What $1,000 a month from affiliates alone would take:** about **83,000 visitors a month** (~2,800 a day), roughly 330 times today.

**Sensitivity:** if clickers buy more full bottles and the average commission rises to $8, it would take about 31,000 visitors a month. If the click rate is 15%, divide either figure by 1.5.

**A mixed path** (still assumptions) needs fewer visitors:
- 1,500 subscribers with 2% on Insider: 30 × $5 = $150 a month
- 2 sponsor slots: about $300 a month
- Affiliates: about $550 a month, which needs roughly 45,000 visitors a month at $12 per 1,000

Either way, traffic has to grow by about 100 times or more. Search is the long-term engine but is slow for a 9-day-old domain. A social channel that actually posts is the only fast lever.
