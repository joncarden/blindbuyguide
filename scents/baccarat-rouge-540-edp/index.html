What wearers say

Wearers say BR540 is a long-lasting, room-filling amber that people either adore or find sickly sweet, metallic or overused, with little middle ground.

Longevity
Most report 8 to 12 hours, with scent lingering on clothes for days; a few say it vanishes quickly or they go nose-blind.
Projection
Typically described as strong and diffusive, a cloud that others notice more than the wearer does.
Compliments
Some wearers say it reliably draws compliments and questions, though others hear it called too sweet or feminine.
Best worn
Cool-weather evenings and special occasions; reads as sweet, airy and unisex, and wearers say apply sparingly.
The common complaints
  • Cotton-candy sweetness gets cloying for some
  • Some smell sharp, metallic, latex or plastic notes
  • Smells overused now; headaches or nose-blindness from heavy application
  • Price and ubiquity
How divisive
