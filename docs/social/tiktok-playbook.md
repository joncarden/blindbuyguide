# TikTok Slideshow Playbook — Blind Buy Guide

Source: Martin Haas (@paco_roadto10), "the complete guide to TikTok slideshows" (X article, Nov 16 2026, from Rocketclip's 10k-slideshow database / 60M+ views). Adapted for blindbuyguide.com. Companion to `docs/social/swipe-file.md`.

Use this doc as the source of truth whenever generating TikTok (or IG carousel) slideshows.

## Why slideshows
- Cheapest repeatable channel: a few photos + a few lines of text. No filming, no face.
- Fast feedback: test 5 hooks on the same slides in a week; saves tell us which problem people care about and how they phrase it.
- Our fit is strong: fragrance buying is a problem people already talk about, advice fits on 5–6 slides, and the site naturally shows up as one tip (price per ml, sample-first verdicts, dupes).

## Step 1 — Strategy: situations, not audiences
Describe the user by the moment right before they need us. Start with 3:
1. About to blind buy an expensive bottle ($150–$400) they've never smelled.
2. Loved a sample / tester and wondering if the full bottle is worth it.
3. Want a famous scent (e.g. Baccarat Rouge 540, Aventus) without paying full price — dupes / cheaper alternatives.
Other candidates: gift for a partner, first niche fragrance, building a 3–5 bottle wardrobe, "smells expensive on a budget", seasonal (date night, office, summer).

Each situation = a series. Keep only situations we've seen in real comments/Reddit; cut ones AI invents.

## Step 2 — Account
- Decision pending (Jon): brand account (@blindbuyguide) vs persona account (a person in the niche, e.g. "stopped wasting money on blind buys").
- If persona: name/pfp of a person and a vibe, bio says who it's for in 2 seconds, post a few slideshows with no site mention first, then bring the site in on slide 4.
- Voice: copy how fragrance commenters talk (from comments under hook-bank posts) — their words, slang, complaints.
- No fake review-style posts with made-up results; those read as ads and get flagged.
- One account until a format works; then clone to more niche angles.

## Step 3 — Hook bank
- Search TikTok for niche keywords (blind buy, niche fragrance, perfume dupes, smell expensive, fragrance tier list). Open 5+ posts per account; note formats that recur across accounts.
- Log every format in a table: link | account | hook | slide order | visuals | where product shows up | comments | views | date.
- Target: 20 formats before writing our own.
- Breakdown prompt (attach screenshots):
  > here are screenshots of a TikTok slideshow. break down the hook, what makes people curious, the order of the slides, how each image matches its text, and where the product shows up. keep what you can see separate from your guesses about why it performed.
- Reuse the structure; write our own words, use our own photos.

## Step 4 — Slides and copy
Order:
- Slide 1: hook — a list or promise worth saving.
- Slides 2–3: value.
- Slide 4: Blind Buy Guide as one tip ("I check price per ml on blindbuyguide.com before I buy").
- Slides 5+: more value, so the site isn't the last thing they see.

Example hooks:
- "5 niche fragrances that are actually safe to blind buy"
- "Stop blind buying these 4 hyped perfumes (sample first)"
- "Baccarat Rouge 540 smell-alikes ranked by price per ml"
- "Buy / sample first / skip: TikTok's most hyped scents"
- "Guess the price: which of these smells most expensive?"

Comment bait: rankings people argue with, tier lists missing someone's favorite, "what would you add?".

Photos:
- Each photo matches its slide's line; leave room for text.
- Same font and text placement across a series.
- NO AI images — real bottle shots, flat lays, shelves, hands. Build a photo library.

Human check before every post: read every slide on a phone. Photo matches line? Text readable? Anything that looks AI or off-brand? Would you save it?

## Step 5 — Testing
- One variable at a time: same slides, 5 hooks, one post a day.
- Judge on saves and comments, not views.
- Weekly review of the table: keep what got saved, cut the rest, update the template.
- Turn a winning format into a reusable spec (inputs, approved examples, past corrections, review checklist). Batch a week of posts in one sitting. Remix formats that blow up in other niches.

## Step 6 — Conversion
- Say the name the way people search it: "Blind Buy Guide" / blindbuyguide.com, on the slide or in the caption, plus link in bio.
- Landing must match the promise: a slideshow about BR540 alternatives links to that page, not the homepage.
- Reply to every "what site is this?" comment — that's the comment we're designing for.

## Ideation prompt (run against our site)
> here's my site: https://www.blindbuyguide.com — it tells you whether a fragrance is safe to blind buy, with honest verdicts plus sample and bottle prices (price per ml) for niche and designer fragrances. list 10 situations my user is in right before they need it. for each one, write how they'd describe it in their own words, and 3 slideshow ideas (list or promise format) where the site could be one of the tips. flag which ideas sound like an ad.

## 3-week plan
- Week 1: write the 3 situations into a strategy doc; set up 1 account; build a 20-format hook bank.
- Week 2: break down top 5 formats; make 5 slideshows (same slides, 5 hooks); post one a day with the human check.
- Week 3: review saves/comments; keep the winning hook, write 5 new slideshows around it; make sure linked pages match each promise.
- After: codify the winner, batch weekly, add a second account once the format holds.
