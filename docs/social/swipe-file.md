# Blind Buy Guide: Social Swipe File

Brief for creating social posts for Blind Buy Guide (https://www.blindbuyguide.com), @blindbuyguide on Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest. Researched Nov 16, 2026. Online version: https://cursor.com/canvas/store-602ccacd-0987-4e18-94f8-44c7b8908677/dc91d155-ec7c-4207-8630-5e7773e0d421

15 faceless formats that already get big numbers in fragrance and nearby niches, with hooks written for Blind Buy Guide and the first 5 posts to make on Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest.

## How to use this file

- Faceless formats only. Never plan talking heads, creator face-to-camera, 'get ready with me' or person-led unboxing. Hands, products, text, charts, illustrations and AI scenes are fine.
- Build every post from the site's own data: verdicts (buy / sample first / skip), bottle and sample prices, price per ml, price history, dupe pairs, notes and the quiz. Fill bracketed placeholders like [$X], [N], [Fragrance] with real values from the price book on the day you post. Never invent a price, ratio or claim.
- Label AI imagery. Any AI-generated scene, render or ingredient image gets an AI label (e.g. 'AI-generated' on the slide or in the caption). Use real bottle photos for reviews and verdicts; keep AI for moods, ingredients and concepts, and never present an AI scene as a real event.
- Add an affiliate disclosure (#ad or 'affiliate links') on posts that link to shop pages.
- Pick a format below, follow its 'How to make it' and 'Hook pattern', and adapt one of its hooks or a template from the hook templates section. Example accounts are for reference only: borrow structures, never their footage, audio or artwork.
- Numbers in this file come from the source named beside them. 'x usual' means a post beat that account's own typical reach by that much; 'x followers' means views divided by followers.

## Faceless works in fragrance

Source: Aubrium, 700+ viral fragrance Instagram Reels from accounts with 1K to 100K followers, Oct 2026. 'x' means views divided by followers.

- 45%: viral reels with no face
- 22x vs 9x: faceless vs face reach (middle reel, views per follower)
- 35x: slideshows, the furthest-travelling format

- 91%: carry on-screen text

Faceless reels went further than face-on ones, and slideshows (TikTok photo mode, Instagram carousels) travelled furthest of all. That suits a data-led brand account. Small accounts break out too: 43% of the viral reels came from accounts under 10K followers.

## Start here: top 5 formats per platform

The edge: No big fragrance account does honest price-per-ml data well. Lead with price gaps, verdict tier lists and one-chart posts, which only Blind Buy Guide's data can make, and use the softer formats (mood boards, memes) for reach.

### Instagram

1. Price-gap dupe carousel: original vs dupe, price per ml, verdict
2. Verdict tier list carousel: buy, sample first, skip
3. One-chart data post: 'niche costs [N]x more per ml than designer'
4. Persona carousel: 'I want to smell like...' at 3 budgets
5. ❌/✅ blind-buy mistakes, plus a weekly collector meme

### TikTok

1. Photo-mode tier list of one house, ranked by verdict
2. Wordless dupe reveal: big price, small price, verdict
3. 'Guess the price' game, answer on the last slide
4. Mood-board slideshow: 'what December smells like'
5. Seasonal countdown: '5 safe winter blind buys under $60'

### Pinterest

1. Note pyramid pin per fragrance, linking to its verdict page
2. 'Smells like' dupe comparison pin with both prices
3. Seasonal and gift edits, posted 30 to 60 days ahead
4. Layering combo pins (Pinterest's 'scent stacking' trend)
5. Price-per-ml cheat sheet and a quiz pin linking to the site quiz

Suggested rhythm for month one: 5 TikTok slideshows, 3 Instagram carousels and 10 to 15 pins a week, all from one shared template set. Each Instagram carousel can be reposted as a TikTok slideshow and split into pins.

## The formats

### 1. Price-gap dupe cards (this vs that)

- **Platforms:** TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest
- **What it looks like:** Two bottles side by side on a plain backdrop. The expensive one's price goes up first, then the cheaper match, then a verdict. No talking, music plus on-screen text.
- **Best platform:** TikTok photo mode, then Instagram carousel
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **How to make it:** Claude writes the pairs and copy from the price book; product cutouts from retailer images or your own sample photos; Canva or Figma template. A few hours for a batch of 10.
- **Data to use:** Bottle price, sample price, price per ml, dupe pairs, verdicts.
- **Hook pattern:** Anchor on the big price, reveal the small one, hold the verdict for the last slide.

**Why it works:** The price gap creates stakes before anything is named. Volume found 'cheap pick that beats the luxury one' worked for 14 beauty accounts (median 4x their usual reach), and 'show the luxury price before testing the dupe' worked for 5 (median 6x). Aubrium counts dupes as the third most common viral fragrance idea (96 creators, 13x followers).

**Examples:**

- [Kutii Essence (@kutifragrance)](https://www.tiktok.com/@kutifragrance): TikTok · wordless 'smells like' reveals · 22.1K followers, 106K median views, standout post 1.2M (Klint, Oct 2026)
- [YK.Scents (@yk.scents)](https://www.tiktok.com/@yk.scents): TikTok · numbered dupe lists · 118K followers, standout post 961K views (Klint, Oct 2026)
- [Dossier (@dossierperfumes)](https://www.tiktok.com/@dossierperfumes): TikTok · 'smells like' brand built on dupe framing · 541.3K followers (TokGauge, Nov 2026)
- [@beauty_time09](https://www.tiktok.com/@beauty_time09): TikTok slideshow 'Expensive VS Cheap' · 16x the account's usual views (Volume, Nov 2026)

**Hooks for Blind Buy Guide:**

- This [$X] bottle smells like a [$Y] one. Here's the price per ml.
- Don't pay [$X] for [Original] until you see this.
- Same vibe, [N]x cheaper per ml.
- We priced [N] dupes of [Original]. Only one earned a blind-buy verdict.
- Original vs dupe: which one would you blind buy?

### 2. Tier lists and ranked countdowns

- **Platforms:** TikTok, Instagram
- **What it looks like:** A grid of bottle cutouts dropped into rows (S to F, or Buy / Sample first / Skip). Or a countdown from 5 to 1 with one bottle per slide.
- **Best platform:** TikTok (slideshow or fast cutout video)
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **How to make it:** Fully templatable. Claude picks the bottles and tiers from verdicts; one tier-board template reused forever.
- **Data to use:** Verdicts map straight onto tiers. Rank by price per ml for a second axis.
- **Hook pattern:** Name the pile and the rule, then make people wait for the top slot.

**Why it works:** Lists are the biggest viral fragrance format: 34% of 700+ viral reels in Aubrium's library. One budget tier list from a 2,476-follower account got 5.1M views. Volume's 'pop-up product cutouts' format (bad / better / best grids too fast to read) hit a median 18x usual reach because people pause and screenshot.

**Examples:**

- [YK.Scents (@yk.scents)](https://www.tiktok.com/@yk.scents): TikTok · rapid tier-by-tier dupe rankings · 118K followers (Klint)
- [Aubrium: budget tier list case](https://aubrium.com/blog/how-to-go-viral-in-fragrance): Instagram Reel from a 2,476-follower account, 5.1M views (Aubrium, Oct 2026). Account not named in the report.
- [Volume: pop-up product cutouts](https://tryvolume.app/working/beauty/pop-up-product-cutouts-built-to-be-screenshotted): 5 beauty accounts, median 18x usual reach (Volume, Nov 2026)

**Hooks for Blind Buy Guide:**

- Blind buy tier list: [House] edition
- Every [designer] bestseller ranked: buy, sample first, or skip
- Ranking [N] viral fragrances by price per ml
- The only S-tier blind buy under $[X]
- Your favorite is in F tier. Fight us.

### 3. Persona and 'which one are you' carousels

- **Platforms:** TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest
- **What it looks like:** Slide 1 says 'I want to smell like...'. Each next slide is one vibe (old money, clean girl, dark feminine) with 2 to 3 bottles and prices, often split into budget, mid and luxury.
- **Best platform:** TikTok photo mode and Instagram carousel
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **How to make it:** AI mood images or bottle cutouts plus a text template. Claude drafts personas and picks matches from verdict data.
- **Data to use:** Scent families, verdicts, price tiers, dupes for the budget slot.
- **Hook pattern:** Identity first, product second, price third.

**Why it works:** People find themselves in a slide and save or send it. Volume measured this format working for 4 accounts at a median 12x their usual reach. A single flat lay titled 'you smell clean & fresh' with the caption 'which one are you?' got 409.4K views (Viral Hooks).

**Examples:**

- [@ellaxcharlotte_](https://www.tiktok.com/@ellaxcharlotte_): TikTok slideshow 'soft feminine...' · 36x usual views, 46K followers (Volume, Nov 2026)
- [@ga.sniffs](https://www.instagram.com/ga.sniffs/): Instagram slideshow 'I Wanna Smell Like..' · 4.0x usual likes, 61K followers (Volume)
- [@axvanillax](https://www.instagram.com/axvanillax/): Flat lay 'you smell clean & fresh', 409.4K views, 27.3K likes (Viral Hooks)

**Hooks for Blind Buy Guide:**

- I want to smell like... old money (and what it costs per ml)
- Which one are you? Clean, sweet, or dark
- Smell like a [persona] at every budget: $, $$, $$$
- Your safest blind buy, based on your vibe
- Pick a slide. That's your next sample.

### 4. Scent mood boards ('what X smells like')

- **Platforms:** TikTok, Pinterest, Instagram
- **What it looks like:** A moment or feeling as the title ('looking at old photos', 'the last day of school'), then a grid of note icons or images: paper, iris, amber, musk. Often built from Fragrantica note images, set to slowed music.
- **Best platform:** TikTok slideshow and Pinterest
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **How to make it:** Easy with AI images or note icons. Use your own note artwork rather than copying Fragrantica's images.
- **Data to use:** Notes per fragrance; the last slide names a bottle with its verdict and sample price.
- **Hook pattern:** A relatable moment, translated into notes, ending on a real bottle.

**Why it works:** The Guardian (Mar 2026) covered these carousels as a big, nostalgic TikTok trend. They are pure feeling, no face needed, and easy to share. Pinterest Predicts 2026 reports searches for 'perfume notes' up 80% and 'signature scent' up 45%.

**Examples:**

- [Fragrantica (@fragranticaofficial)](https://www.instagram.com/fragranticaofficial/): Instagram · the note database these boards are built from · 340.6K followers (PicMany, Oct 2026)
- [The Guardian on fragrance mood-board TikToks](https://www.theguardian.com/culture/2026/mar/23/tiktok-perfume-trend-fragrance-pictures-mood-boards): Trend write-up, Mar 2026 (no view counts given)
- [Pinterest Predicts 2026: Scent Stacking](https://www.gcimagazine.com/consumers-markets/news/22957242/scent-stacking-to-glitchy-glam-pinterest-predicts-cosmetic-and-fragrance-trends-for-2026): Search growth for niche perfume, layering and notes (GCI Magazine)

**Hooks for Blind Buy Guide:**

- What a first date in December smells like
- What 'expensive but quiet' smells like
- If [city] were a fragrance
- What Sunday morning smells like, and the bottle that nails it
- What your 2016 Tumblr smelled like

### 5. Note breakdowns and ingredient macros

- **Platforms:** Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest
- **What it looks like:** Bottle hero shot, then extreme close-ups of each ingredient (lavender, resin, citrus peel) in the same color grade. Or a bottle with animated top, heart and base note overlays.
- **Best platform:** Instagram Reels
- **Difficulty:** Medium
- **How to make it:** AI image or video tools handle ingredient macros well. Keep the real bottle as a photo and generate only the ingredients around it.
- **Data to use:** Note pyramids, longevity, 'smells like' descriptors, verdict as the last frame.
- **Hook pattern:** Show the bottle, break it into parts, put it back together.

**Why it works:** It turns an invisible product into something you can see. Dior's 12-second Sauvage Elixir macro montage got 2.7M views. Sunnies Face's faceless note guide (bottle, spray, note overlays) got 1.4M views, 231x the account's usual. Aubrium: 'fragrance notes explained' reels travel 12x followers.

**Examples:**

- [Sunnies Face (@sunniesface)](https://www.instagram.com/sunniesface/): Faceless scent-note guide, 1.4M views, 231x usual (Viral Hooks)
- [Viral Hooks: Sauvage Elixir breakdown](https://viralhooks.org/database/18888-cinematic-ad-showcasing-fragrance-ingred/): Shot-by-shot analysis of the macro format

**Hooks for Blind Buy Guide:**

- What's actually in [Fragrance], note by note
- Why [Fragrance] smells different after 2 hours

- Top, heart, base: what [$X] gets you

### 6. Data charts and price-per-ml posts

- **Platforms:** Instagram, Pinterest
- **What it looks like:** One headline that states the takeaway, one simple bar or line chart, one highlight color, gray for the rest, small source line. Sneaker and watch accounts do the same with resale prices.
- **Best platform:** Instagram carousel (1080 square or 4:5)
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **How to make it:** Claude can generate charts straight from the price dataset (code or a chart tool) in one house style. Lowest-effort, highest-moat format.
- **Data to use:** Price book, price per ml, price history, sample vs bottle math.
- **Hook pattern:** The headline is the conclusion; the chart is the proof.

**Why it works:** This is the format nobody in fragrance owns yet, and it is Blind Buy Guide's unfair advantage. Chartr grew a 500K-subscriber newsletter on headline-first, low-clutter charts (OpenData Labs). StockX posts plain price updates, like the Travis Scott AJ1 'Velvet Brown' averaging $462 after release, to 3.7M Instagram followers. Chrono24 publishes its ChronoPulse watch price index.

**Examples:**

- [Chartr (@chartrdaily)](https://www.instagram.com/chartrdaily/): Instagram · headline-first business charts · newsletter of 500K+ subscribers (OpenData Labs, Mar 2026)
- [Visual Capitalist (@visualcap)](https://www.instagram.com/visualcap/): Instagram · one-idea data graphics · about 409K followers (Instagram profile)
- [StockX (@stockx)](https://www.instagram.com/stockx/): Instagram · resale price posts · about 3.7M followers (HypeAuditor, Oct 2026)
- [Chrono24 (@chrono24)](https://www.instagram.com/chrono24/): Instagram · watch market and price index · about 772K followers (InstMe)

**Hooks for Blind Buy Guide:**

- Niche costs [N]x more per ml than designer. Is it worth it?
- The cheapest way to own [Fragrance], charted
- Sample vs full bottle: the point where the math flips
- [Fragrance] got [N]% cheaper this year
- Where $100 goes furthest: price per ml by house

### 8. Studio bottle shots and editorial still life

- **Platforms:** Instagram, Pinterest
- **What it looks like:** One bottle on a flat color backdrop, hard shadow, or a sculptural still life with props and texture. Minimal copy. This is the grid look of Le Labo, Byredo and Diptyque.
- **Best platform:** Instagram grid
- **Difficulty:** Medium
- **How to make it:** Medium. Use real photos of real bottles (your samples or licensed retailer images). AI renders of branded bottles can get the bottle wrong, which hurts trust on a review site.

- **Hook pattern:** Minimal text: a verdict stamp or a price per ml on a beautiful frame.

**Why it works:** It sets the brand's taste and makes the grid look premium, which helps every other post convert. Le Labo has about 2.1M Instagram followers (Jolygram), Diptyque about 1.7M (Instagram profile), and Byredo is large (trackers range from 764K to 941K).

### 10. Quizzes, guessing games and brackets

### 12. Stop motion, papercraft and animated explainers

- **Platforms:** Instagram, TikTok
- **What it looks like:** Paper or clay dioramas that show where an ingredient comes from, or 2D animation that explains a concept. Stick-figure animation works too.
- **Best platform:** Instagram Reels
- **Difficulty:** Hard
- **How to make it:** Real stop motion is slow. AI video can fake a papercraft look for short explainers; keep them under 30 seconds.
- **Data to use:** Explain why niche costs more, concentration (EDT vs EDP), price per ml.
- **Hook pattern:** Ask how a raw thing becomes a product, then answer it piece by piece.

**Why it works:** It feels handmade in a feed full of slick ads. Ffern's papercraft 'How does an orange tree become a fragrance?' got 855.5K views and 56.3K likes. Dior's animated Miss Dior story got 6.9M views. Fits By Stick grew a fragrance YouTube channel on stick-figure buying guides (76K subscribers, 85.2K median views, Klint).

### 13. CGI and AI 'impossible' visuals

- **Platforms:** TikTok, Instagram
- **What it looks like:** A giant bottle in a real city (fake out-of-home), a fragrance as a room or landscape, a surreal product world. Short and loopable.
- **Best platform:** TikTok and Instagram Reels
- **Difficulty:** Hard
- **How to make it:** AI video tools make this possible for a small team, but quality takes iteration. Label it as AI, and don't present invented scenes as real events.
- **Data to use:** Best used on 'If [Fragrance] were a place' or seasonal hero posts, not on price claims.
- **Hook pattern:** Show something impossible in a familiar place, then tie it to the scent.

**Why it works:** Surprise drives shares. Maybelline's CGI mascara train passed 74M views, 2.8M likes and 831K shares (Social Amour) and cost about $30,000 (Campaign US). Jacquemus's rolling bags got over 2M Instagram likes (Dialect). Adopt Parfums made 'Don't Touch My Mini' with AI tools, no shoot days, and reached 2M views in 48 hours (Higgsfield).

**Examples:**

- [Maybelline (@maybelline)](https://www.tiktok.com/@maybelline): TikTok · CGI mascara train, 74M+ views (Social Amour); 76M (Echo Poster)

### 14. Relatable memes for collectors

- **Platforms:** Instagram, TikTok
- **What it looks like:** Text-over-image jokes about collecting, blind buys gone wrong, and the bank account after a discovery set. Classic meme templates with fragrance captions.
- **Best platform:** Instagram feed and Stories
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **How to make it:** Very easy. Claude drafts 20 captions a week; you pick the funny ones. Stick to templates you can legally use.
- **Data to use:** Light touch: the punchline can be a price-per-ml fact.
- **Hook pattern:** Name the thing every collector does but won't admit.

**Why it works:** Aubrium found relatable hooks went furthest of the common hook types (21x followers). 'Only people that collect fragrances will understand' got 1.8M views from a 12K account. Memes are cheap to make and get shared into group chats.

### 15. Wrong way vs right way (❌ / ✅) cards

- **Platforms:** TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest
- **What it looks like:** A red ❌ over the mistake (buying 100ml first, spraying on clothes, trusting one review), then a green ✅ with the fix. Works as cards or as a hands-only video.
- **Best platform:** TikTok and Instagram carousel
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **How to make it:** Easy template; Claude drafts the mistakes from the site's buying advice.
- **Data to use:** Sample vs bottle math, verdict methodology, price-per-ml traps.
- **Hook pattern:** Call out a common mistake, then show the fix.

**Why it works:** Volume measured 'red ❌ wrong way, then green ✅ fix' working for 14 beauty accounts at a median 9x usual reach. Aubrium's 'Stop Applying Your Perfumes Like this❌' reel got 583K views from an 11K account.

## Guardrails

- Check every dupe and price claim against the price book on the day you post; prices move.
- Add an affiliate disclosure (#ad or 'affiliate links') on posts that link to shop pages.
- Use real bottle photos for reviews. Label AI scenes as AI, and keep AI to moods, ingredients and concepts.
- Use your own note artwork and chart style rather than copying Fragrantica's or Chartr's look.
- Borrow structures from face-on creators (games, countdowns), never their footage or audio.
