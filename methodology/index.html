How we track fragrance prices

Short version: every price on Blind Buy Guide was read from a public retailer page on the date shown next to it. Price per ml is price divided by milliliters. The library covers 291 fragrances and was last refreshed 2026-11-15.

What we measure

Lowest sample. The lowest total price for a decant or sample of the fragrance among the sample retailers we track, with the size and retailer named.

Best bottle. The lowest price per ml among full bottles we saw marked in stock. Sold-out listings stay visible for context but never set the best bottle.

Price per ml. Listed price divided by size in ml, rounded to the cent. Prices are in US dollars and exclude tax and shipping.

Where the numbers come from

Retailer product pages: authorized luxury retailers for full bottles where available, plus decant and sample retailers for samples. Each scent page links the exact page we read. Notes, accords, house and concentration come from published brand and retailer information.

How fresh it is

Every price shows its check date. We refresh the library and publish a new Price Book edition monthly. Prices move, and a number we published can be wrong by the time you read it, which is why the date is always beside it.

What we do not do

We do not claim to have smelled or worn any fragrance, and we do not accept payment for verdicts. Some links are affiliate links, as described in our affiliate disclosure and editorial policy. The site is written with AI tools, explained on how we use AI.

Download and cite the data

The Price Book is free. The same figures are available as CSV and JSON. To cite: Blind Buy Guide, Price Book, 2026-11-15 edition, https://blindbuyguide.com/price-book/.

Frequently asked questions

How does Blind Buy Guide calculate price per ml?

Price per ml is the listed price divided by the bottle or sample size in milliliters, for example $360 for 70 ml is $5.14 per ml. We show it for every size we track so a 2 ml sample and a 100 ml bottle can be compared on the same footing.

How often are the prices updated?

Every price on a scent page carries the date it was checked. We refresh the price library and publish a new edition of the Price Book each month, so a price older than that should be treated as out of date.

Does Blind Buy Guide test the fragrances?

No. We do not claim to have smelled or worn anything. Notes and accords come from published brand and retailer information, the analysis is price and sampling math, and the site is written with AI tools, as explained on the how we use AI page.

Which retailers do the prices come from?

Full-bottle prices come from the retailer pages we link on each scent page, with authorized luxury retailers such as Bergdorf Goodman and Neiman Marcus preferred where they carry the fragrance. Sample prices come from decant and sample retailers, which are independent resellers rather than the brands themselves.

Can I cite or reuse the Blind Buy Guide price data?

Yes. Cite it as Blind Buy Guide, Price Book, with the edition date and the link https://blindbuyguide.com/price-book/. The same figures are available as CSV and JSON.
