Before you buy

Five rules for gifting fragrance

  1. Snoop first. Look at what's already on their dresser, which candle they keep rebuying and which of your sweaters they keep borrowing. Fragrance taste leaves evidence. Our printable version has a twelve-question snoop sheet for exactly this.
  2. Fragrance is a blind buy on someone else's skin. The same bottle can read creamy on one person and sharp on another. That's why the smartest gift in this guide is a set of samples plus a promise: they test, they choose, you buy the bottle.
  3. 50 ml is the gifting size. A 50 ml bottle is roughly 500 sprays, enough to fall in love without a year-long commitment. Go to 100 ml only when they already wear it.
  4. Buy from an authorized retailer. Department stores and brand boutiques give you gift wrap, a gift receipt and a bottle that's definitely real. Grey-market discounts are for your own blind buys, not someone else's present.
  5. Order early. Samples ship from small decanters and bottles sell out in December. Ordering by the first week of December leaves time to swap a guess that missed.

Questions

Gifting fragrance, answered

Is perfume a good gift?

Perfume is a good gift when you know the person's taste or give them a way to choose. Because a scent smells different on each person's skin, a sample set followed by the full bottle they pick is the lowest-risk way to gift luxury fragrance.

What size perfume bottle should I give as a gift?

Give a 50 ml bottle for a scent they haven't worn before; it holds roughly 500 sprays. Give 100 ml only if they already wear and love that fragrance, since the bigger bottle usually costs less per ml.

How do I choose a fragrance for someone else?

Start from evidence: the fragrances, candles and body products they already buy. Fresh and citrus scents are the easiest to like; sweet, smoky and oud scents are more personal. When in doubt, gift three to five samples and let them pick the bottle.

Is a perfume sample set a good gift?

Yes. A set of 1 to 2 ml samples lets someone test each fragrance for several days on their own skin before committing to a full bottle. Pair it with a card promising the bottle of their choice.

Can you return a perfume gift?

Return rules for fragrance vary by retailer, and many only accept unopened bottles. Check the retailer's policy before you buy and ask for a gift receipt.

When should I order fragrance for the holidays?

Order by the first week of December. Samples ship from smaller decanters and popular bottles sell out, so an early order leaves time to swap a guess that missed.
