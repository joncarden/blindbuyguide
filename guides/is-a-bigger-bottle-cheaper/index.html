Is a bigger perfume bottle actually cheaper?

Short answer: Yes, per ml a larger bottle is usually much cheaper. On Nov 14, 2026, Parfums de Marly Delina cost $8.33 per ml for 30 ml but $5.47 per ml for 75 ml at the same retailer, both in stock.

Usually yes, and the gap is bigger than most people expect. Small bottles carry the same marketing, glass and box as the big ones, so you pay a premium per ml for less juice.

Brands know you're too scared to commit. They charge you for it.

When to size up

  • You've worn it at least five or six times (a 2 ml sample plus a few tester days) and still want it.
  • You'd wear it weekly or more. At 4 sprays a wear, 100 ml lasts about 250 wears.
  • It's a signature, not a mood. Seasonal or special-occasion scents rarely justify 100 ml.

When small (or a decant) wins

  • You want variety more than volume. Five 10 ml decants beat one 100 ml bottle for a rotation.
  • It's a strong scent you use 1 or 2 sprays of (looking at you, Baccarat Rouge 540).
  • You're not sure yet. That's what samples are for: which sample size to buy.

Verdict: if you love it and wear it a lot, size up and stop paying the commitment-issues tax.
