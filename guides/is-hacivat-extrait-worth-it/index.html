What it smells like, per the note list

The notes as listed: top: Pineapple, Grapefruit, Bergamot; heart: Cedar, Patchouli, Jasmine; base: Oakmoss, Woody notes.

The notes are pineapple, grapefruit and bergamot, then cedar, patchouli and jasmine, over oakmoss and woody notes. Wearers commonly describe it as fruity but greener and sharper than its famous relative.

Who it's worth it for, and who should pass

Worth it if: you like Aventus in theory and want something greener and sharper in practice.

Pass if: you want sweet or dark. This one is bright, fruity and woody.
