What it smells like, per the note list

The notes as listed: top: Palo Santo, Incense, Violet Leaf, Black Pepper; heart: Papyrus, Leather, Labdanum, Rose, Cade oil; base: Ebony trees, Resins, Guaiac Wood.

Wearers commonly describe something smoky, leathery and incense-heavy. It is very The Brutalist: monumental, moody and not for everyone. The note list reads dark and woody.

Who it's worth it for, and who should pass

Worth it if: you want a smoky, incense-forward woody with leather, for cooler weather and moody rooms.

Pass if: you want sweet or bright. This one smolders.
