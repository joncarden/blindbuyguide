What the gaps tell you

  • Never buy from the first shop you see. On a $400 bottle, five minutes of comparison can be worth $50 to $140.
  • A sold-out deal is not a deal. Decant and discount shops often list full bottles they rarely restock. Compare against what you can actually buy today.
  • Department stores and brand sites usually charge list price. You pay for the official channel and easy returns.
  • The same shop can be cheapest on one scent and priciest on another, so compare per scent, not per shop.
