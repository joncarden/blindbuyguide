Is Another 13 worth it?

Short answer: Sample it first: Le Labo Another 13 has a 1 ml sample at $7.99 at Fragrances Line on Nov 14, 2026, and we have no US dollar bottle price in our library.

Le Labo Another 13 is a sample-first purchase, and a mandatory one: a 1 ml sample is $7.99 at Fragrances Line as of Nov 14, 2026. It is the is-it-even-on scent, in a good way. Some people barely smell it on themselves, which is an expensive discovery to make at full price.

What it smells like, per the note list

The notes as listed: top: Pear, Apple, Citruses; heart: Ambrette (Musk Mallow), Amyl Salicylate, Moss, Jasmine; base: Iso E Super, Cetalox, Helvetolide, Ambrettolide.

The notes are pear, apple and citruses, then ambrette, amyl salicylate, moss and jasmine, over Iso E Super, Cetalox, Helvetolide and ambrettolide. Wearers commonly describe quiet, skin-close musks, and reviews are split on how much it projects.

Who it's worth it for, and who should pass

Worth it if: you like skin-close musks and want a scent that reads as you, only better. Quiet luxury, if you want a reference.

Pass if: you want a scent that arrives before you do.
