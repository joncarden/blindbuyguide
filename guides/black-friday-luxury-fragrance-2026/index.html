Black Friday luxury fragrance 2026: what is actually on sale

Short answer: Luxury fragrance houses almost never cut the sticker price for Black Friday, and when we rechecked on Black Friday, Nov 27, 2026, they still had not. The real savings are store codes: Bergdorf Goodman's BGBEAUTY takes $50 off $250 of regular-price beauty (so a $300 Tom Ford Oud Wood 50 ml lands at $250), Neiman Marcus's THANKFUL does the same for beauty (Tom Ford Tobacco Vanille 50 ml, $300, lands at $250), and Bluemercury's free BlueRewards program lists 20% off with any purchase on Parfums de Marly Delina ($250 for 30 ml becomes $200). A 1 ml sample of any of the 10 most-searched bottles still costs $4.99 to $9.99.

Luxury fragrance houses almost never cut the sticker price for Black Friday, and on Nov 23 and Black Friday, Nov 27, 2026, they had not. What moves is the store around the bottle: a spend-and-save code, a member tier, a gift card. We read the retailer pages for the 10 most-searched luxury bottles on our site, wrote down every price with its date, then checked again on Black Friday itself, Nov 27. Two things changed, both at the register: Neiman's product page now spells out its code tiers (and they include beauty), and Bluemercury dropped the $200 minimum on its 20%. The bottle prices did not move, and neither did a single one of the 132 sample and bottle prices we logged at the sample shops. Consistency: the one thing in luxury fragrance that never goes on sale.

The three codes worth knowing

Bergdorf Goodman, BGBEAUTY. $50 off $250, $125 off $500, $250 off $1,000 and $625 off $2,500+ on regular-price beauty. One $300 bottle earns the first tier. Two bottles at $250+ each clear $500 and save $125, which is the closest thing to a real discount we found on Tom Ford Private Blend. Bergdorf is also running a gift card event on other departments; read the terms before assuming it covers fragrance.

Bluemercury, BlueRewards. On Black Friday the tiers read 20% off any purchase, 25% off $800+ and 30% off $1,500+ on the Delina page we read (on Nov 23 the 20% needed $200). BlueRewards is free to join. On Delina, 20% turns a $410 bottle of 75 ml into $328, or $4.37 per ml, lower per ml than any in-stock Delina in our library.

Neiman Marcus, THANKFUL. $50 off $250, $125 off $500, $250 off $1,000, $500 off $2,000, $1,000 off $3,500 and $1,500 off $5,000 of regular-price purchases, and the page says it includes beauty. A $300 Tobacco Vanille lands at $250. Free shipping on $150+ with SHIPNM.

What we rechecked on Black Friday

  • The sample shops (Fragrances Line and Scent Split, top 20 scents): all 132 sample and bottle prices we logged on Nov 23 were unchanged on Nov 27, and nothing went in or out of stock.
  • Bergdorf Goodman: Oud Wood 50 ml still $300, BGBEAUTY unchanged.
  • Neiman Marcus: Tobacco Vanille 50 ml still $300; THANKFUL tiers now listed, beauty included.
  • Bluemercury: Delina still $250 and $410; BlueRewards 20% no longer needs $200.
  • Not re-read on Nov 27: Baccarat Rouge 540 and Lost Cherry at Bergdorf, Santal 33 at Luckyscent, Gypsy Water at Bluemercury. Their rows keep the date we last read them.

The honest math

The best Black Friday saving on this list is $82 on a 75 ml Delina. A 1 ml sample is $7.99. If you have not worn the scent for a full day, the sample is the better use of money, because the most expensive bottle is the one you stop wearing in February. If you already know it is your signature, this week is a fine time to buy it, and the codes above are the ones to use.
