Price per ml is the only fair way to compare a 30 ml bottle with a 125 ml one. Price divided by size. That's it. Divide by 10 again and you get the rough cost per spray.

What we found (US dollar listings not marked sold out, Nov 14, 2026)

  • Median full bottle: about $3.90 per ml, or 39 cents a spray.
  • Median 2 ml sample: $12.99, about $6.50 per ml.
  • Median 1 ml sample: $6.99.

How to use the number

  • Comparing shops: same size, lower per ml wins. See the same perfume at different prices.
  • Comparing sizes: the bigger bottle almost always wins per ml. See is a bigger bottle cheaper?
  • Comparing samples to bottles: samples cost more per ml. You're paying for the right to say no.

Verdict: under $3 per ml for niche, the math is mathing. Over $5, you'd better be obsessed.
