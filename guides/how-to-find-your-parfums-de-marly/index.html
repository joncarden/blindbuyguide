A sampling plan

1. Order four 1 ml samples (about $30): Layton, Herod, Delina and one wildcard.

2. Test two at a time, one per wrist, over two days. How to test properly.

3. Upgrade the winner to 2 ml before buying, and wear it in different weather.

4. Buy the 125 ml if it exists. At this house the big bottles are often the best value per ml.

Quick notes from the note lists

  • Layton: apple, lavender, bergamot up top; vanilla, cardamom and sandalwood underneath.
  • Herod: cinnamon and pepperwood, then tobacco leaf and incense, on vanilla and cedar.
  • Delina: lychee, rhubarb and Turkish rose, on cashmeran and vetiver.
  • Sedley: citrus and rosemary, lavender, then sandalwood and vetiver. The fresh one.
  • Haltane: lavender, clary sage, then praline and saffron, on cedar and oud.

Verdict: never blind buy PdM. Thirty dollars of samples will save you a $300 horse.
