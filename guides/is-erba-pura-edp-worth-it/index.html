What it smells like, per the note list

The notes as listed: top: Sicilian Orange, Calabrian bergamot, Sicilian Lemon; heart: Fruits; base: White Musk, Madagascar Vanilla, Amber.

The notes are Sicilian orange, Calabrian bergamot and Sicilian lemon, a fruit heart, then white musk, Madagascar vanilla and amber. Wearers commonly describe it as loud, juicy and sweet. It is the Real Housewives reunion of scents: fun, high-volume, and not subtle.

Who it's worth it for, and who should pass

Worth it if: you want juicy, sweet citrus that fills a room, and a scent that behaves like a personality.

Pass if: you want something quiet, or you share an open-plan office with people who notice.
