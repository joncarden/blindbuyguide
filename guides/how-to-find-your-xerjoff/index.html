A Xerjoff sampling plan

  • The fresh box: Erba Pura and Torino 21. About $17 for 1 ml each.
  • The warm box: 1861 Naxos and Alexandria II. About $16.

Test two at a time over a couple of days (how to test properly), then move the winner to a 2 ml before you even look at a bottle.

Notes, per the brand and retailers

  • Erba Pura: Sicilian orange, Calabrian bergamot and Sicilian lemon; fruits; white musk, Madagascar vanilla and amber.
  • 1861 Naxos: lavender, bergamot and lemon; honey, cinnamon, cashmeran and jasmine sambac; tobacco leaf, tonka bean and vanilla.
  • Torino 21: mint, lemon, basil and thyme; black currant, lavender, rosemary and jasmine; lemon verbena and musk.
  • Alexandria II: cinnamon, lavender, rosewood and apple; cedar, lily of the valley and rose; amber, vanilla, musk, sandalwood and oud.

Verdict: Alexandria II at $523.50 for 100 ml is a lot to commit to on a note list. Sample it first.
