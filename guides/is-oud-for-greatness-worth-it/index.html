What it smells like, per the note list

The notes as listed: top: Saffron, Nutmeg, Lavender; heart: Agarwood (Oud); base: Patchouli, Musk.

The note list is saffron, nutmeg and lavender, then oud in the heart, over patchouli and musk. Wearers commonly describe it as bold and statement-making. The oud sits dead center, which is the point.

Who it's worth it for, and who should pass

Worth it if: you want a statement oud and are comfortable being the loudest person at dinner.

Pass if: you are new to oud, or you want a scent that stays out of the conversation. The softer door is Oud Wood.
