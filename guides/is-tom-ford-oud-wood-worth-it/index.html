What it smells like, per the note list

The notes as listed: top: Exotic rosewood, Cardamom, Chinese pepper; heart: Rare oud wood, Sandalwood, Vetiver; base: Tonka bean, Vanilla, Amber. One retailer's notes disagree (Chinese pepper in one list, pink pepper and patchouli in another), so check the brand page before you quote it at a party.

Wearers commonly describe it as smooth and polished, a refined oud with rosewood and cardamom up front and tonka, vanilla and amber underneath. It is regularly recommended as an oud for people who think they do not like oud.

Who it's worth it for, and who should pass

Worth it if: you want to try oud without the animal-in-the-room reputation, and you like woods with a little sweetness. It plays well in an office.

Pass if: you want your oud loud and unapologetic. This is the Soho House version, tasteful and a little tame.
