How to find your Le Labo before you commit

Short answer: The smartest way to try Le Labo is 1 ml decants, which cost $7.99 each at Fragrances Line on Nov 14, 2026, before you commit to a full bottle.

Le Labo is the house of lab-style labels and a cult following, and the one house in this series where we have no US bottle price to quote. Nobody should choose one off a note list. A flight of 1 ml samples lets you find your Le Labo before you commit to it.

A Le Labo sampling plan

  • The one box: Another 13, Bergamote 22 and Rose 31. About $24 for 1 ml each.

Test two at a time over a couple of days (how to test properly), then move the winner to a 2 ml before you even look at a bottle. Santal 33 has no US sample price in our data yet, so check its page for updates.

Notes, per the brand and retailers

  • Another 13: pear, apple and citruses; ambrette, amyl salicylate, moss and jasmine; Iso E Super, Cetalox, Helvetolide and Ambrettolide.
  • Santal 33: Australian sandalwood, Virginia cedar, cardamom, violet, papyrus, leather, amber and iris.

Our library has no note list for Bergamote 22 or Rose 31, so we are not going to guess. Another 13 reads like a call sheet for a Severance-style lab: mostly molecules, very few flowers.

Verdict: with no US bottle price on file, the only number worth trusting is $7.99 for a 1 ml. Sample it first.
