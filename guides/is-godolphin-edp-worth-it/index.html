What it smells like, per the note list

The notes as listed: top: Thyme, Saffron, Raspberry; heart: Rose, Iris, Jasmine; base: Leather, Ambrox, Blond Woods. Retailers disagree slightly: one lists thyme, saffron and cypress on top and a leather accord, so check the brand page.

Wearers commonly describe a saffron-and-leather scent with a fruity raspberry twist. It is a Wes Anderson production design in a bottle: precise, a little odd, very specific. The note list is unusual enough that reviews are split.

Who it's worth it for, and who should pass

Worth it if: you like leather and saffron and are curious about a raspberry twist.

Pass if: you want a safe crowd-pleaser. The list is unusual, and unusual is the point.
