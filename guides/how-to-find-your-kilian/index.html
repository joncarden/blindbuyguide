A By Kilian sampling plan

  • The gourmand box: Angels' Share and Black Phantom. About $15 for 1 ml each.
  • The floral box: Good Girl Gone Bad and Love, Don't Be Shy. About $16.

Test two at a time over a couple of days (how to test properly), then move the winner to a 2 ml before you even look at a bottle.

Notes, per the brand and retailers

  • Angels' Share: cognac; cinnamon, oak and tonka bean; praline, vanilla and sandalwood.
  • Good Girl Gone Bad: osmanthus, jasmine and May rose; Indian tuberose and narcissus; amber and cedar.
  • Love, Don't Be Shy: neroli, bergamot, pink pepper and coriander; orange blossom, jasmine, honeysuckle and rose.

Our library has no note list for Black Phantom, so we will not guess. Angels' Share reads like the bar cart in a Soho House members' room: cognac, oak, cinnamon.

Verdict: a $415 listing that was sold out when we checked is a poor basis for a decision. Sample it first.
