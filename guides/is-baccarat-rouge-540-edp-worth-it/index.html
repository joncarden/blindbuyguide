What it smells like, per the note list

The notes as listed: top: Saffron, Jasmine; heart: Amberwood, Ambergris, Hedione; base: Fir resin, Cedar, Sugar, Ambroxan, Oakmoss.

The accords on the page are breeze (jasmine and saffron), heat (cedarwood) and mineral (ambergris). Wearers commonly describe it as airy, sweet and a little mineral, and it is instantly recognizable, hence the sample. If you want to smell like a secret, this is not it.

Who it's worth it for, and who should pass

Worth it if: you like airy, sweet and mineral, and you do not mind being recognized by strangers on elevators. It is a crowd-pleaser, so go in knowing the crowd has already met it.

Pass if: you want something nobody else in your office wears. Recognizability is the product here.
