How to find your Byredo before you commit

Short answer: The smartest way to try Byredo is 1 ml decants, which cost $6.99 each at Fragrances Line on Nov 14, 2026, against bottle listings of $210 to $330.

A Byredo sampling plan

  • The clean and quiet box: Blanche and Mojave Ghost. About $14 for 1 ml each.
  • The outdoors box: Gypsy Water and Bal d'Afrique. About $14.

Test two at a time over a couple of days (how to test properly), then move the winner to a 2 ml before you even look at a bottle. Super Cedar has no US sample or bottle price in our data yet.

Notes, per the brand and retailers

  • Gypsy Water: bergamot, lemon, pepper and juniper berries; incense, pine needles and orris; amber, vanilla and sandalwood.
  • Mojave Ghost: sapodilla and ambrette; magnolia, violet and sandalwood; ambergris, cedar and musk.
  • Blanche: white rose, pink pepper and aldehydes; violet, orange blossom and peony; light woods, sandalwood and musk.
  • Super Cedar: rose; Virginian cedar; Haitian vetiver and musk.

Our library has no note list for Bal d'Afrique, so we are not going to guess. Mojave Ghost's list is giving a minimalist Architectural Digest spread: very few elements, all of them chosen.

Verdict: Gypsy Water spans $210 to $330 in the listings we found, and the lower one was sold out. Sample it first, then buy from a retailer you trust.
