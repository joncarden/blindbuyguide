What it smells like, per the note list

The notes as listed: top: Peach, Blood Orange, Cardamom, Heliotrope; heart: Rum, Cognac, Davana, Jasmine; base: Sandalwood, Indonesian Patchouli Leaf, Cashmeran, Tonka Bean, Vanilla, Styrax, Labdanum, Benzoin, Vetiver.

The notes are peach, blood orange, cardamom and heliotrope, then rum, cognac, davana and jasmine, over sandalwood, patchouli, cashmeran, tonka, vanilla, styrax, labdanum, benzoin and vetiver. Wearers commonly describe a boozy, sweet, loud peach. It is Call Me By Your Name with a rum chaser.

Who it's worth it for, and who should pass

Worth it if: you want a statement dessert scent and are happy for it to be a conversation.

Pass if: you want fresh or subtle. Short list of fans is part of the product.
