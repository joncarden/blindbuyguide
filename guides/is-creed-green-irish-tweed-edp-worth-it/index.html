What it smells like, per the note list

The notes as listed: top: Bergamot, Lemon, Peppermint; heart: Geranium, Lavender; base: Oakmoss, Woods. Retailers disagree on the notes (the brand page and two retailers each list different ones), so the list above follows the Creed brand page.

Wearers commonly describe a classic green and clean scent: lemon and peppermint up top, geranium and lavender, then oakmoss and woods. It is the fragrance equivalent of a well-pressed blazer.

Who it's worth it for, and who should pass

Worth it if: you want a classic fresh green scent with some heritage, and you like the idea of owning the one Aventus fans graduate from.

Pass if: you want something dessert-sweet or dark. This is crisp and green.
