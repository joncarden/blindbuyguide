How to find your Tom Ford before you commit

Short answer: The smartest way to try Tom Ford is 1 ml decants, which cost $3.99 to $7.99 at Scentsplit, Scent Split and Fragrances Line on Nov 14, 2026, against bottle listings of $165 to $445.

A Tom Ford sampling plan

  • The leather box: Tuscan Leather and Ombré Leather. About $12 for 1 ml each.
  • The dark florals box: Black Orchid and Noir de Noir. About $12.
  • The smoky box: Tobacco Vanille and Oud Wood. About $14.

Test two at a time over a couple of days (how to test properly), then move the winner to a 2 ml before you even look at a bottle. Lost Cherry has no US sample or bottle price in our data yet.

Notes, per the brand and retailers

  • Tobacco Vanille: tobacco leaf and spicy notes; vanilla, cacao, tonka bean and tobacco blossom; dried fruits and woody notes.
  • Tuscan Leather: raspberry, saffron and thyme; olibanum and jasmine; leather, suede, woody notes and amber.
  • Oud Wood: exotic rosewood, cardamom and Chinese pepper; rare oud wood, sandalwood and vetiver; tonka bean, vanilla and amber.
  • Black Orchid: French jasmine, black truffle, ylang-ylang, black currant and citrus; patchouli, sandalwood, dark chocolate, incense, amber, vetiver, vanilla and balsam.
  • Lost Cherry: bitter almond, liquor and black cherry up top, with sour cherry, plum, Turkish rose and jasmine sambac in the heart.

Reading that Tobacco Vanille list is like watching the first ten minutes of a Succession finale: a lot of money, a lot of warm wood, and no idea yet who comes out ahead. The drydown is where you find out.

Verdict: Tom Ford is not a house to blind buy, and the Private Blend prices are not a rounding error. Sample it first.
