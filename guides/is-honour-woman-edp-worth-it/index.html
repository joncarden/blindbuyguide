What it smells like, per the note list

The notes as listed lean floral and resinous: pepper, rhubarb leaves and coriander open it, with jasmine, tuberose, gardenia, lily of the valley and carnation in the heart. The base is vetiver, frankincense, amber, opoponax and leather, so the white florals sit on a dry, resinous, leathery base.

The notes as listed: top: Pepper, Rhubarb leaves, Coriander; heart: Jasmine, Tuberose, Gardenia, Lily of the valley, Carnation; base: Vetiver, Frankincense, Amber, Opoponax, Leather. Retailers sometimes list notes differently, so check the brand page before you buy.

Who it's worth it for, and who should pass

Worth it if: you want a full white floral that ends in leather and resin, and you're sure the floral-and-leather combination is yours.

Pass if: you want a soft, simple floral; the tuberose, carnation and leather make this a layered, heavy one.
