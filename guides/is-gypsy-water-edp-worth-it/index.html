What it smells like, per the note list

The notes as listed: top: Bergamot, Lemon, Pepper, Juniper berries; heart: Incense, Pine needles, Orris; base: Amber, Vanilla, Sandalwood.

Wearers commonly describe a woodsy, calm scent: citrus and juniper up top, incense and pine needles in the heart, amber, vanilla and sandalwood underneath. The note list reads like a quiet cabin and not a bonfire.

Who it's worth it for, and who should pass

Worth it if: you want woodsy and calm, with a bit of incense. It suits a person with a good throw blanket and no desire to explain it.

Pass if: you want something sweet and loud. This one is more hush than headline.
