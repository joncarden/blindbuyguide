What it smells like, per the note list

The notes as listed lean sweet and spiced: cinnamon, honey and osmanthus open the scent, with benzoin, ambergris, labdanum and artemisia in the heart. Vanilla, tonka bean, patchouli and musk make the base warm and resinous.

The notes as listed: top: Cinnamon, Honey, Osmanthus; heart: Benzoin, Ambergris, Labdanum, Artemisia; base: Vanilla, Tonka bean, Patchouli, Musk. Retailers sometimes list notes differently, so check the brand page before you buy.

Who it's worth it for, and who should pass

Worth it if: you want a honeyed, cinnamon-warm scent with a resinous base, a cashmere throw in fragrance form.

Pass if: sweet and spiced sounds like too much; with honey, vanilla and tonka in the mix, this leans rich.
