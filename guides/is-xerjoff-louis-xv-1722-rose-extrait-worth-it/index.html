What it smells like, per the note list

The notes as listed lean fruity and rosy, with blackcurrant, raspberry, pear and rose on the list beside geranium. Cedarwood, musk, vanilla and amyris add warmth and woods, so the list is not all fruit and petals.

The notes as listed (the source gives one flat list, not a top, heart and base pyramid): Blackcurrant, Raspberry, Pear, Geranium, Cedarwood, Rose, Musk, Vanilla, Amyris. Retailers sometimes list notes differently, so check the brand page before you buy.

Who it's worth it for, and who should pass

Worth it if: you want blackcurrant, raspberry and pear with rose and geranium, and cedarwood and vanilla for warmth

Pass if: you want a fresh, green scent, since the list is fruit, rose, musk and vanilla
