A Creed sampling plan

  • The "is it worth the hype" box: Aventus, Green Irish Tweed, Viking. About $19 for 1 ml each.
  • The fresh box: Silver Mountain Water, Original Vetiver, Erolfa. About $18.
  • The fancy box: Royal Oud, Wind Flowers, Millésime Impérial. About $25.

Test two at a time over a couple of days (how to test properly), then move the winner to a 2 ml before you even look at a bottle.

Notes, per the brand and retailers

  • Green Irish Tweed: bergamot, lemon, peppermint; geranium and lavender; oakmoss and woods.
  • Silver Mountain Water: bergamot, mandarin, neroli; green tea and blackcurrant bud; sandalwood and musk.
  • Original Vetiver: ginger, mandarin, bergamot; vetiver, sandalwood, iris; musk and ambergris.
  • Viking: bergamot, lemon, pink pepper; peppercorn, rose, peppermint; sandalwood, vetiver, patchouli, lavender.

Verdict: a $495 blind buy is a Succession-level power move with none of the Succession-level money. Sample it first.
