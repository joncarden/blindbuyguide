How to find your Maison Francis Kurkdjian before you commit

An MFK sampling plan

  • The "is it worth the hype" box: Baccarat Rouge 540 and Aqua Universalis. About $16 for 1 ml each.
  • The evening box: Oud Satin Mood, Oud Silk Mood and Gentle Fluidity Gold. About $30.

Test two at a time over a couple of days (how to test properly), then move the winner to a 2 ml before you even look at a bottle. Grand Soir has no US sample or bottle price in our data yet, so it sits this round out.

Notes, per the brand and retailers

  • Baccarat Rouge 540: saffron and jasmine; amberwood, ambergris and hedione; fir resin, cedar, sugar, ambroxan and oakmoss.
  • Gentle Fluidity Gold: juniper berries and nutmeg; coriander; vanilla, musk, caramel and amberwood.
  • Aqua Universalis: bergamot and lemon; freesia and lily of the valley; light musks.
  • Oud Satin Mood: Bulgarian rose, violet and strawberry; Turkish rose; oud, vanilla, amber, benzoin, caramel and cedar.

At $584 for 200 ml, Oud Satin Mood is a considered purchase, and the one listing we found in stock. That is a Met Gala seating-chart level of commitment, so test it first.

Verdict: Baccarat Rouge 540 is a $270 listing that was sold out when we checked, so $9.99 for a 1 ml is the sensible opening move. Sample it first.
