How many sprays are in a 2 ml perfume sample?

Short answer: A 2 ml sample holds roughly 20 sprays from a typical atomizer, which is enough to wear a fragrance 4 to 6 times at 3 to 5 sprays a wear.

Enough to know whether you love it or whether it was just the store lighting.

A typical sample atomizer releases about 0.1 ml per spray, so every 1 ml is roughly 10 sprays. Spray sizes vary by nozzle, so treat these as estimates.

Is 2 ml enough to decide on a full bottle?

Usually, yes. Four to six wears lets you try a fragrance on different days and in different weather, check how long it lasts on your skin, and see whether you still like the drydown (the base notes, after several hours) on the third day as much as the first. A 1 ml vial often runs out before you know.

What does a sample cost per spray?

Sample prices in our library (checked Nov 14, 2026) have a median of $12.99 for 2 ml, so about 65 cents a spray. That is far more per spray than a full bottle: Baccarat Rouge 540 was listed at $270 for 70 ml at Fragrances Line (sold out when we checked), about 39 cents a spray. The premium buys you the right to change your mind.

Making a sample last

  • Spray on skin, not clothes, when you are testing: fabric changes how a scent develops.
  • Use 2 sprays the first time. You can always add a third.
  • Keep vials out of the bathroom and away from sunlight; heat and light break fragrance down.
