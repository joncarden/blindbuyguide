A blind buy is safe when the price is low enough to shrug off and the people who wear it mostly agree. Out of 299 fragrances in our price library, exactly three purchases pass that test today, and two of them are boxes of minis. That's not us being dramatic. That's the data.

## Our test

We call a purchase a safe blind buy only if all three are true:

- **The price is near or under our $150 line** for a full bottle, or it is a discovery set (minis are the sampling, gift-wrapped). See [should you blind buy a niche fragrance?](/guides/should-you-blind-buy-niche-fragrance/) for where the line comes from.
- **Wearers mostly agree.** We read Reddit, Basenotes, Fragrantica and TikTok comments for 25 popular scents and rate how divided opinion is. A safe blind buy needs low division, not just high ratings.
- **We have a dated, US dollar price** from the retailer.

Most of the library fails the first test: the median in-stock bottle in our [November price index](/guides/blind-buy-guide-price-index-november-2026/) is $4.78 per ml, about $478 for 100 ml. Where we haven't read wearer opinion yet, a scent can't pass the second, however good the reviews you've seen.

## The list

### 1. Tom Ford Private Blend Luxe Discovery Set: $100

Four Private Blend minis of about 3 ml each: Lost Cherry, Soleil Blanc, Oud Wood and Tobacco Vanille. It is $100 at Bluemercury (Nov 18, 2026), or $8.33 per ml. Our verdict is **buy it**: the lowest-priced way into the line, and it answers four "should I buy the bottle?" questions at once. [Full verdict](/guides/is-tom-ford-private-blend-luxe-discovery-set-worth-it/).

### 2. Creed Men's 5-Piece Discovery Set: $380

Five Creed minis of about 9.8 ml each: Aventus, Absolu Aventus, Silver Mountain Water, Millésime Impérial and Green Irish Tweed (check the listing; the second bottle has changed between print runs). It is $380 at Bluemercury (Nov 18, 2026), or $7.76 per ml. Not small money, but Aventus alone is listed at $495 for 100 ml (sold out when we checked). Our verdict is **buy it**, as a set to find out which Creed is yours before the $400-plus bottle. [Full verdict](/guides/is-creed-mens-5-piece-discovery-set-worth-it/).

### 3. Tom Ford Ombré Leather Eau de Parfum: $165 for 50 ml, if you like leather

The one full bottle that comes close. Wearer opinion is unusually consistent: in the comments we read, the occasional dislike is about leather itself, not the quality, and most report 8 to 12 hours of wear. It is $165 for 50 ml on the Tom Ford Beauty site (stock not confirmed, Nov 14, 2026), $15 over our line. The notes list cardamom, leather, jasmine sambac, amber, moss and patchouli. **Safe blind buy only if you already like leather scents.** If you're not sure, the 1 ml sample is $4.99 at Scentsplit and the [worth-it guide](/guides/is-ombre-leather-edp-worth-it/) has every price.

## Close, but not on the list

- **Maison Francis Kurkdjian Grand Soir.** Wearer opinion is overwhelmingly positive, and the complaints are about sweetness and price. But the only US dollar listing we have is $275 for 70 ml at Harrods with stock not confirmed, and we have no sample source yet. [Its page](/scents/grand-soir-edp/).
- **Tom Ford Costa Azzurra.** $165 for 50 ml, in stock at Bluemercury (Nov 14, 2026), but we haven't read wearer opinion on it yet, so it can't pass the second test. Sample it: 1 ml is $9.99 at Fragrances Line. [Its page](/scents/tom-ford-costa-azzurra-edp/).
- **Nishane Safran Colognise.** $100 for 100 ml, in stock at Luckyscent (Nov 14, 2026), the lowest-priced in-stock full bottle in our library. We don't have a confirmed note list yet, and blind buying a scent nobody can describe is just buying a bottle. [Its page](/scents/nishane-safran-colognise/).

## Famous, and not safe to blind buy

These are the bottles people blind buy most, and the reviews we read are sharply split on every one. Sample first.

- **Baccarat Rouge 540:** $360 for 70 ml at Bergdorf Goodman (Nov 16, 2026). 1 ml sample $9.99. [Page](/scents/baccarat-rouge-540-edp/).
- **Tom Ford Tobacco Vanille:** $300 for 50 ml at Neiman Marcus (Nov 16, 2026). 1 ml sample $6.99. [Page](/scents/tom-ford-tobacco-vanille-edp/).
- **Tom Ford Lost Cherry:** $405 for 50 ml at Bergdorf Goodman (Nov 14, 2026), and performance reports range from 20 minutes to all day. [Page](/scents/tom-ford-lost-cherry-edp/).
- **Tom Ford Black Orchid:** $165 for 50 ml at Sephora, near our line, but wearers split between devotion and "harsh." 1 ml sample $3.99. [Page](/scents/black-orchid-edp/).
- **Le Labo Santal 33:** wearers split sharply over one note in particular. 1 ml sample $7.99. [Page](/scents/santal-33-edp/).

**Verdict:** the safest blind buy is a box of minis. The second safest is a bottle you can already describe from the scents you love. Everything else is a sample first.

We update this list when prices or wearer data change. We don't smell or handle the bottles we cover; wearer opinion comes from the public reviews linked on each scent's page.
